The problem: your best people spend their day copying and pasting

New lead comes in — someone retypes it into the CRM. Job finishes — someone remembers (or forgets) to send the invoice. A week passes — nobody asked the happy customer for a Google review. None of it is hard. All of it eats hours, and every manual step is a place where things fall through the cracks.

Small businesses lose deals not to worse service but to slower follow-up. The business that responds to an inquiry in five minutes wins it; the one that responds tomorrow doesn't hear back.

What we build

  • Instant lead follow-up — every form fill, call, or email inquiry gets an immediate, personalized response and lands in your CRM with the next step scheduled.
  • Client intake that runs itself — forms, document collection, reminders for the stragglers, and a clean file when it's done.
  • Invoicing & payment chasing — invoices go out when the work closes; polite reminders go out until they're paid.
  • Review generation — happy customers get a well-timed text asking for a Google review. This alone changes a local business's trajectory.
  • Document & email handling — AI that drafts the routine emails, summarizes the long threads, and files what comes in.

Common questions

What's the difference between this and hiring a virtual assistant?

A VA costs $1,500+ a month forever, works set hours, and takes vacations. An automation is built once, runs around the clock, never forgets a follow-up, and you own it. Many clients use both — automation for the repetitive work, humans for judgment calls.

Do I have to change the software I use?

No — that's the point of custom development. We build around your existing tools rather than forcing you onto a new platform.

How do I know which workflows are worth automating?

That's exactly what the free assessment finds. The rule of thumb: anything done more than five times a week, the same way, by copying information from one place to another, is a candidate.

What does workflow automation cost?

Each automation is scoped and priced individually in your itemized plan — you approve every line before we build. Simple automations are surprisingly affordable; start with one and add more as each proves itself.
