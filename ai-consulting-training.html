The problem: everyone says "use AI," nobody says how

You've read the headlines. Maybe someone on your team uses ChatGPT for emails. But between "we should be doing something with AI" and actually saving ten hours a week, there's a gap — and most of what's sold to fill it is either enterprise consulting you don't need or a subscription to yet another tool nobody opens after week two.

What we offer

  • AI roadmap sessions — we sit down with you, map how your business runs, and deliver a prioritized, honest list: what to automate now, what to wait on, and what to ignore entirely. Fixed fee, no obligation to build with us.
  • Hands-on team training — a working session built around your team's real tasks. Your estimator learns to draft estimates with AI; your office manager learns to clear the inbox. Everyone leaves with prompts and habits they'll actually use Monday morning.
  • Tool selection, minus the hype — we don't sell software or take referral fees, so you get a straight answer on what's worth paying for.
  • Safe-use policies — plain-English rules for what can and can't go into AI tools. If you handle client files, patient information, or case details, you need this before your team improvises it.

Who this fits

The owner who wants a plan before spending

A roadmap session turns "we should do something with AI" into a short, prioritized list with real costs attached — often the first item pays for the session many times over.

The team that bought tools nobody uses

Training built on your actual workflows is the difference between a subscription that lapses and a team that's measurably faster.

The firm that's worried about risk

Law offices and medical practices get the guardrails first — confidentiality-safe workflows and a use policy — then the productivity.

How pricing works

Consulting and training are the exception to "it depends": they're fixed-fee engagements, quoted up front. And every engagement starts the same way as everything else we do — with a free 30-minute assessment to make sure it's worth your money before you spend any.

Common questions

Do I need consulting, or should I just have you build something?

If you already know your bottleneck — missed calls, slow follow-up — skip straight to the free assessment for a build. Consulting is for when you know AI should be helping but can't see where to start, or when your team needs to get productive with AI tools themselves.

What does the training actually cover?

Whatever your team actually does. We build the workshop around your real workflows — writing estimates, answering client emails, drafting content — not generic "intro to ChatGPT" slides.

Can you help us with an AI use policy?

Yes — a plain-English policy covering what your team may and may not put into AI tools, client confidentiality, and review requirements. Especially relevant for law firms and medical practices.

What does consulting cost?

Roadmap engagements and workshops are fixed-fee and quoted up front in your itemized plan — no open-ended hourly meters.
