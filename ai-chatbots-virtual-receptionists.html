The problem: your phone doesn't stop, but your team does

You're with a customer. It's after hours. The front desk is at lunch. The phone rings four times and goes to voicemail — and industry data says most callers won't leave one. They just call the next business on the list.

For a Tampa law office, that's a signed case lost. For a restaurant, it's a party of twelve booking somewhere else. For a plumber, it's an emergency job — the most profitable call you get — gone in forty seconds.

What we build

  • AI phone receptionist — answers every call in one ring, any hour. Handles hours, directions, pricing questions, and books appointments straight onto your calendar.
  • Website chat that actually converts — not a dumb FAQ widget. It qualifies the lead, answers real questions about your business, and captures contact info before the visitor leaves.
  • Missed-call text-back — can't answer? The caller instantly gets a text: "Sorry we missed you — want to book online or have us call back?" Most lost calls become saved leads.
  • Smart handoff — routine calls handled by AI; anything complex transferred to a human or summarized to your phone in seconds. You set the line.

Built around your business, not a template

Off-the-shelf bots know nothing about you. Ours is trained on your services, your prices, your policies, your tone — and wired into the tools you already use: your scheduling system, your CRM, your practice management software. When it books a consult, the appointment is on your real calendar with a real confirmation sent.

What it looks like in a business like yours

Law office

After-hours callers reach a receptionist that collects the case type, urgency, and contact details, books the consult, and flags emergencies to the on-call attorney — instead of a voicemail box that fills up.

Restaurant or venue

The AI takes reservation calls during the dinner rush your hosts can't get to, answers "do you have outdoor seating?" for the hundredth time, and texts large-party inquiries to the manager.

Home services

Emergency calls at 11pm get answered, triaged, and booked — with the after-hours premium quoted — while your competitors' phones ring out.

Medical or wellness practice

Appointment requests, rescheduling, and new-patient questions handled around the clock, so the front desk starts the day with a schedule instead of a voicemail queue.

Common questions

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

We configure it however you're comfortable — most businesses have the AI introduce itself honestly, and most callers don't mind because it answers instantly, never puts them on hold, and gets them booked. You'll hear it yourself before it ever takes a live call.

What happens with complicated calls?

The AI handles the routine — hours, directions, booking, common questions — and hands anything complex to a human: transfer, detailed message, or an instant text summary to you. You decide where that line is.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

It depends on call volume and what it needs to do. After the free assessment and deep dive, you get an itemized plan with exact pricing before anything is built — typically a one-time build cost plus modest monthly usage.

Does it work with my booking system?

Almost certainly. We build custom integrations with the calendar, CRM, or scheduling tool you already use — that's the point of hiring developers instead of buying an off-the-shelf bot.
